05 / HOW I WORK

Evidence first.
Decisions second.

Good engineering isn't just a working query. It's a clear problem, a reliable process and results you can explain.

01 / UNDERSTAND
◎

Start with context.

Clarify what the business needs, define the questions and understand the limits of the data.

02 / VALIDATE
⌘

Trust, then verify.

Check assumptions, document rules and test for missing, duplicated or inconsistent records.

03 / ENGINEER
⟐

Build for reliability.

Use maintainable SQL, Python and analytical models, then test each change against expected behavior.

04 / COMMUNICATE
↗

Make it useful.

Turn technical results into understandable insights, accessible reports and decisions people can act on.
